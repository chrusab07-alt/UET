    Transport Rules for Students

    • Always display your valid UET Student Transport Pass when boarding.
    • Arrive at your assigned pickup stop at least 5-10 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
    • Maintain discipline and keep the university buses clean.
    • For any urgent emergency or schedule query, contact the Chairman Transport Committee.
